Problem-Based Learning Theory: The Visual Hierarchy in Deep CNNs

1. Receptive Fields & Depth

As layers deepen, receptive fields expand exponentially. Early filters observe local 3x3 pixel neighborhoods; late layers integrate global contextual relationships across the entire image.

2. Representation Disentanglement

ReLU non-linearities drive activation sparsity (typically 40-70%), carving orthogonal representations that separate distinct visual semantics and reject background noise.

3. Faithfulness vs Interpretability

Grad-CAM localizes high-level discriminative regions; Saliency highlights pixel boundaries. Combining both verifies whether the model reasons via true morphology or spurious shortcuts.
